3. Types of Information Collected

ZPIN collects various categories of personal and non-personal information from Users in order to provide, operate, improve, and secure its services.

ZPIN collects personal information that is voluntarily provided by Users at the time of registration, account creation, order placement, or interaction with the Platform. 3.6 Information from Third-Party Sources. ZPIN may receive information about Users from third-party sources, including sellers, delivery partners, payment processors, analytics providers, and marketing partners.
